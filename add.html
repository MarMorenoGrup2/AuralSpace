Configuration of the room

This tool allows for the extrapolation of the auralization method to any space by uploading custom 3D models and Impulse Responses.

  • Model 3D in format .GLB
  • IRs in .WAV (mono/estéreo)
  • Required Naming: IRs must include the audio mode in their name (MONO, STEREO, or BINAURAL) and be ordered by rows (F) and seats (A) from 1 to 5.
    Example: MONO-1F-1A.wav.
